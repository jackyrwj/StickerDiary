FAQ

Where are my stickers and diary pages stored?

Only on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, so deleting the app removes them.

Do my photos leave my phone?

No. Stickers are cut out on your device.

What does Pro include?

Every paper theme, every handwriting font, and the monthly recap you can share.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?

Cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. To restore on a new device, open the Pro page in the app and tap Restore Purchases.
