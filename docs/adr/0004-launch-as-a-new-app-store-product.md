# Launch as a new App Store product

The personal-sticker product will use a new App Store record and bundle identifier instead of replacing the existing Sticker Diary binary in place. The former app has no meaningful installed base to preserve, while the new product has a different purpose, data model, privacy story, monetization surface, and brand promise.

## Consequences

The existing Sticker Diary listing will be removed from sale but not deleted from App Store Connect. The new product requires its own name, product page, privacy disclosure, in-app purchases, screenshots, review notes, and launch analytics; it will not inherit the former app's store URL or installed users.
